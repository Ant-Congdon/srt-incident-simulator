Before you begin

You are a tier 1 analyst on the service desk at South Regional Technologies. A ticket is about to arrive. Work through the incident across six decision points. At each one, type what you would do and, just as importantly, why.

Use the Situation Report and Logbook panels beside the chat as you go. Keep the SitRep current and log every decision with the time, the action and your reasoning.

Safety note: This exercise uses some real malicious indicators so you can practise looking them up in threat intelligence tools. Submit them to AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal, URLhaus or URLScan.io for analysis only. Do not open, browse to, download or run any URL or file. Web addresses are shown defanged (hxxps and [.]) on purpose. Only re-type them inside an analysis tool, never in a browser address bar.
